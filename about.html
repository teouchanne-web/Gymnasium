Our Story

From a single gym to a movement — this is how Gymnasium was born.

How It All Started

Gymnasium was established in 2010 with one simple belief — that everyone deserves access to a world-class fitness experience. What started as a small, passionate team in Tbilisi quickly grew into one of Georgia's most recognized gym networks.

Our founders were athletes themselves. They knew what was missing — not just equipment, but guidance, community, and real results. So they built exactly that. A place where beginners feel welcome and professionals feel challenged.

Over the years, we've expanded across multiple locations, trained thousands of members, and built a team of certified coaches who genuinely care about your progress. But our core hasn't changed — we're still that same passionate team, just bigger.

Where We Are Today

Today, Gymnasium operates across several branches in Georgia, offering state-of-the-art equipment, personal training, group classes, and nutrition coaching — all under one roof. We're proud to be more than a gym. We're a community built on discipline, progress, and support.
